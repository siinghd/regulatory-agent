Privacy notice

This explains what happens to your information when you email {{ p.agent_address }} or open a link it sends you. Questions or requests: {{ p.privacy }}.

What we collect

  • Your email: your address and display name, the subject and text of your message, and its technical headers, which include the IP address of the server that sent it to us and the results of the checks that prove the email really came from you. We don't open attachments.
  • What we did with it: the request we understood, its progress, and the emails we sent you.
  • When you open our links: our web server and our network provider record your IP address, browser, the page and the time.

We don't use tracking cookies, build profiles, or sell or rent your information.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request and send you the documents: that's why you wrote to us.
  • To keep the service safe: we only answer email that provably comes from its sender and we limit how often anyone can use the service, so it can't be used to send mail to people who didn't ask for it.
  • To keep it working and accountable: diagnosing failures and keeping a record of what the system did.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, for it to be corrected or deleted, or for us to stop processing it. To have everything deleted, email {{ p.agent_address }} from the same address with DELETE MY DATA in the subject: we confirm by email, delete everything we hold about that address within 30 days (usually within a day) and stop processing mail from it. For anything else, or if you no longer have that address, write to {{ p.privacy }}; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

We only answer authenticated email, encrypt data in transit, download links and backups, and restrict access to our systems. To report a vulnerability, email {{ p.security }}; please don't test against other people's data or disrupt the service. If something goes wrong that affects you, we'll tell you.
